## Overview

This document outlines the standard operating procedures for Call Closers managing discovery calls, lead tracking, and administrative tasks using the Call Closer spreadsheet, Calendly, and Keap.

## 1. Finding Leads & Using the Call Closer Spreadsheet

### Process

1. **Access the Spreadsheet**
   Open the **TGPC - Closer Calls 2025 Spreadsheet**

2. **Open the Lead List**
   Locate and open the sheet labeled with the correct month (e.g., "March & April 2025")

3. **Call Assigned Leads**
   Begin calling the leads listed in your assigned sheet

4. **Update Columns**
   Update the **DC Next Step Column** and **DC Admin Next Step Column** with the correct status

5. **Update Hot List/Call Out List**
   Indicate follow-up actions and add notes

6. **Save Updates in Keap**
   Use the note template **Next Steps** and add the notes as the description

7. **Repeat for Every Discovery Call**
   Complete steps 4-6 for every discovery call done

8. **Update Outcome Column**
   Indicate the result of the final discovery call

### Note Format

When adding notes in the Call Closer Spreadsheet, use this format:

**Example:** `27/04 Zac - Rescheduled`

## 2. Booking a Discovery Call

### First: Calendly Booking

1. Log in to **Calendly** and select the correct user
2. Select and open the correct event type
3. Enter the correct information in the necessary fields
4. Click **Schedule the Event** to confirm the meeting

### Second: Keap Note Recording

1. Log in to **Infusionsoft/Keap** using your `@thegoodpropertycompany.co.uk` account
2. Search for the lead in Keap by their name or email address
3. Add a note and select the appropriate Discovery Call template:
   - **Booked 1st Discovery Call → Warm Up Pre Call**
   - **Booked 2nd Discovery Call → Warm Up Pre Call**, etc.

#### Important Discovery Call Counting Rules

- Check the lead's history for any previous Discovery Calls **within the last year**
- Discovery Calls count **cumulatively regardless of who delivered them**

**Example:**
- ✔️ 1st Discovery Call with Zac
- ✔️ 2nd with Susannah
- ✔️ 3rd with Zac

These are all counted in sequence as their 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Discovery Calls.

**No-Show/Cancellation Rule:**
If a Discovery Call was previously booked but the client did not attend (no-show, cancelled, or rescheduled), that call **does not count** toward the next number. The count only increases once the Discovery Call has actually been delivered.

4. Add the correct call description using this format:
   `[CALL SETTER'S NAME] / [MONTH] / [DATE] / [TIME]`
   **Example:** `ZAC MARCH 7TH 1PM`

5. Click **Save** to confirm the update in Keap, then record the booking in both the Call Setter and Call Closer spreadsheets

### Third: Book Follow-Up Discovery Call (If Applicable)

If you have booked another Discovery Call with the lead:

1. Add the appropriate Discovery Call template (following the same counting rules above)
2. Use format: `[CALL SETTER'S NAME] / [MONTH] / [DATE] / [TIME]`
3. Click **Save** to confirm and record in spreadsheets

### Fourth: Send GHL Messages

1. Log in to **Go High Level** and find the lead
2. Send the appropriate **SMS, WhatsApp message, and email** templates based on the lead's interests and needs
3. Refer to the conversation and Fathom call summary to determine which template to use

## 3. If Leads Are Late to Joining the Call

### Process

1. **Copy the Zoom link** of the meeting
2. Log in to **Go High Level** and find the lead
3. Send them this **text message** and **email** via GHL:

#### Text Message

```
Hi [Lead Name], I am in our Zoom link now for our property meeting. Here is the link: [Insert Link]. I will wait 10 minutes before jumping out. I hope you can join me, see you soon, [Caller Name]
```

#### Email

**Subject:** `[Lead Name] // Property Meeting – Join Me on Zoom`

```
Hi [Lead Name],

I'm in our Zoom meeting now. You can join using this link: [Insert Link].

I'll wait for 10 minutes before heading out. Hope to see you there!

Best,
[Caller Name]
```

4. After 10 minutes, send another message:
   `I'm jumping out of the meeting...here's the link to rebook...`

## 4. For No Show Discovery Call

### First: Mark as No Show in Calendly

1. Log in to **Calendly** and select the correct user
2. Go to the **Meetings** tab and click on **Past**
3. Find the correct meeting and click **Details**
4. Click **Mark as No Show** to update the meeting status

### Second: Add Note in Keap

1. Log in to **Infusionsoft/Keap** using your `@thegoodpropertycompany.co.uk` account
2. Search for the lead in Keap by their name or email address
3. Add a note and select the template **No Show Discovery Call**
4. Add the correct call description in this format:
   `NAME OF THE PERSON ASSIGNED TO THE CALL / MONTH / DATE / TIME OF THE SUPPOSED CALL`
   **Example:** `ZAC MARCH 7TH 1PM`
5. Click **Save** to confirm the update in Keap, then record the update in the Call Closer spreadsheet

### Third: Add Next Steps (If Applicable)

If there are clear "Next Steps" to do:

1. Add a note and select the template **Next Steps**
2. Add the correct call description:
   `Date - Caller's Name Initials - notes about what was talked about and the next steps to take`
   **Example:** `09/04 JT - Rei is still saving money for the academy. Contact her again on the end of year`
3. Click **Save** and record in spreadsheets

### Fourth: Send GHL Follow-Up Messages

1. Log in to **Go High Level** and find the lead
2. Send appropriate SMS, WhatsApp message, and email

**For WhatsApp**, use these templates:
- Initial: `missed_discovery_call_rebook_Zac_1`
- After 3 days: `missed_discovery_call_rebook_Zac_2`

Refer to the Fathom call summary to determine message content based on lead's interests.

## 7. For Not Interested in Paying for Education

### Process

1. Log in to **Infusionsoft/Keap** using your `@thegoodpropertycompany.co.uk` account
2. Search for the lead in Keap by their name or email address
3. Add a note titled **Not Interested in Paying for Education** and include the original date the client expressed this
4. Click **Save** to confirm the update in Keap, then record the update in the Call Closer spreadsheet

### Important Note

**Do not apply any permanent removal or veto actions** for leads who are simply not interested at the moment. These leads may be open to future contact. Only tag and note them accordingly without removing them from the database or applying permanent tags.

Make sure to note this in the Call Closer spreadsheet so the lead can be included in future follow-ups.

## 9. Note Templates Guide

Apply the correct Note Template based on the situation:

| Template | Use Case |
|----------|----------|
| **Spoke to - Follow Up** | Caller talked to the lead; to take note of call details |
| **Call # - No Answer** | For failed calls/leaving VMs |
| **Not Interested In Paying for Education** | For leads not interested in paid education |
| **Remove from Database** | For leads who don't want to receive marketing messages |
| **Next Steps** | To take note of follow-up actions to do |
| **Veto List** | For rude leads, needs to be added in the veto list |
| **Missing Number - Action Needed** | For leads with wrong contact number |
| **Call - Webinar Registered by Team - Client Request** | For webinar registrations requested through calls |
| **GHL - Webinar Registered by Team - Client Request** | For webinar registrations requested through GHL messages |

**Put X in the cell** to indicate that there would be no follow-up calls to be done.

## 10. Veto List Note

### Scenario

If you encounter rude leads.

### Process

1. Log in to **Infusionsoft/Keap** using your `@thegoodpropertycompany.co.uk` account
2. Search for the lead in Keap by their name or email address
3. Add a note and select the template **Veto List**
4. Add the correct call description:
   `Date - Caller's Name Initials - Call details`
   **Example:** `09/04 JT - Spoke offensive remarks`
5. Click **Save** and record the update in the Call Setter and/or Call Closer spreadsheet with note **Veto List**

### Important

Use this note **only for leads who displayed offensive or disrespectful behavior** during a call. This allows us to keep a record of inappropriate conduct.

**If a lead intentionally drops the call or hangs up**, do not use this note. Instead, record it under the "Next Steps" note and clearly specify the follow-up actions you will take.

## Quick Reference

### Key Platforms

- **TGPC Closer Calls 2025 Spreadsheet** - Lead tracking and call management
- **Calendly** - Discovery call scheduling
- **Infusionsoft/Keap** - CRM and note management
- **Go High Level (GHL)** - Communication platform
- **Fathom** - Call recording and transcription

### Important Reminders

- Always check lead history before selecting Discovery Call templates
- Discovery Calls only count when actually delivered (not just booked)
- Use appropriate message templates based on lead interests
- Update spreadsheets immediately after each interaction
- Never apply permanent removal for leads who are simply not interested at the moment
