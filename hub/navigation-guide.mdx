# TGPC Info Hub Navigation Guide 🧭

This comprehensive guide helps you navigate the TGPC Info Hub efficiently, understand the organization structure, and quickly find the information you need.

## 🗺️ Site Structure Overview

The TGPC Info Hub is organized into **4 main sections** based on how you use the information:

### **🏠 Getting Started**
*Your orientation and navigation center*
- **Hub Overview** - Main dashboard and quick access
- **Navigation Guides** - This guide and SOP-specific navigation

### **📈 Sales & Marketing** 
*Everything for sales activities and marketing*
- **Products & Pricing** - All product information and payment links
- **Free Resources & Lead Generation** - Webinars, resources, and lead magnets
- **External Marketing** - Social media and YouTube content

### **🔧 Operations**
*Internal processes, training, and documentation*
- **Standard Operating Procedures** - All SOPs organized by function
- **Team Operations** - Onboarding, security, and team processes
- **Sales & Lead Management** - Call handling, qualification, scheduling
- **Client Services** - Payment processing and client management
- **Training & Documentation** - Product training and educational materials
- **CRM & Data Management** - Keap tags, notes, and data organization

### **🔗 Resources**
*Quick access links and technical information*
- **Quick Access Links** - Calendly and external booking systems
- **System & Technical** - Tech stack and system documentation

## 🔍 Advanced Navigation Tips

### **Cross-Reference Navigation**
Most SOPs include cross-references to related procedures:
- **Prerequisites** - What to complete first
- **Related SOPs** - Connected procedures with direct links
- **Process Context** - How the SOP fits into larger workflows

**Example:** When viewing [Call Handling](../sops/call-handling), you'll see links to:
- [SPIN Scripts](../sops/spinabt) for lead qualification
- [Calendly Procedures](../sops/calendly-procedures) for scheduling
- [Payment Handling](../sops/payment-handling) for closing sales

### **Breadcrumb Navigation**
Use the breadcrumb trail at the top of each page to:
- **Understand your location** in the site structure
- **Navigate back** to parent sections
- **Jump between** related sections quickly

### **Search Strategies**
When looking for specific information:
1. **Start broad** - Use main section navigation first
2. **Use cross-references** - Follow links between related procedures
3. **Check multiple sections** - Some topics span multiple areas
4. **Use role-based navigation** - Start with your role's quick access

## 🆘 When You Can't Find Something

### **Troubleshooting Navigation Issues:**

#### **"I can't find a specific procedure"**
1. Check the [SOPs Hub](../sops/index) - All procedures are listed there
2. Use the [SOP Navigation Guide](../sops/navigation-guide) - Detailed SOP-specific help
3. Try role-based navigation from the [Hub Dashboard](./index)

#### **"I need something that spans multiple areas"**
1. Start with your primary role's section
2. Use cross-references to find related content
3. Check the hub dashboard for popular destinations

#### **"The structure doesn't make sense to me"**
1. Review this navigation guide completely
2. Start with the [Hub Dashboard](./index) for orientation
3. Use the role-based quick access sections

#### **"I need help with a specific process"**
1. Go to Operations → [SOPs Hub](../sops/index)
2. Use the role-based navigation in SOPs
3. Follow the process flow guidance in relevant SOPs

### **Getting Additional Help:**
- **Process Questions:** Contact operations team
- **Technical Issues:** Check [Tech Stack](../tech-stack) or contact IT
- **Access Problems:** Review [Security Procedures](../sops/security)
- **Training Needs:** Visit [Training Documents](../training-documents/index)
